TLINE Privacy Policy
====================

TLINE is a SIP client for Android. It does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party push
notification services.

SIP credentials, call and message history, contacts created inside TLINE, application settings,
diagnostic logs, and recordings stay on the device unless the user explicitly exports or shares
them. TLINE may read Android contacts only after the user grants permission and only to display or
use them for calls and messages. The application does not upload the address book to the developer.

Calls, messages, registration data, and related network traffic are sent to the SIP provider chosen
by the user. When TLINE is routed through TRELAY, that traffic also passes through the VPN servers
configured by the user in TRELAY. Those services have their own privacy policies and operational
rules.

Removing TLINE deletes its private application data according to Android's normal application-data
rules. Files explicitly exported by the user are not removed automatically.

This policy describes the source tree in this repository and does not override the behaviour or
terms of an independently configured SIP provider, VPN provider, or Android device vendor.
